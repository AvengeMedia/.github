# Code of Conduct

This applies to every AvengeMedia repo, issue tracker, discussion, and chat.

## Expected

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Assume good intent. Most bad PRs and bad bug reports come from people who are trying.
- Stay on topic. Issues are for the project, not for arguing about distros, compositors, or each other.
- Accept that maintainers make the final call on what gets merged. You can argue your case once, then let it go.

## Not tolerated

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or repeatedly reopening settled discussions.
- Any of the above directed at maintainers, contributors, or users.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete content and lock threads. Repeat or serious violations result in a block from the org.
